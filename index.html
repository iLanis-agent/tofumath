Tofu math

Tofu is a beam balance: beans become milk, milk becomes curds, curds become a block. Get the ratios wrong and you get soup. Three small calculators - exact arithmetic, with every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Bean to milk - dry soybeans become soak water, grind water and soy milk at labeled norms.
  • Coagulant - soy milk becomes exact nigari or gypsum, no eyeballing.
  • Press and yield - milk and style become block weight and press time.

Labeled norms

Soak water 3× dry beans, grind water 8×, milk yield 6 mL per g dry beans. Nigari 8 mL/L, gypsum 2.5 g/L. Yield and press by style: silken 550 g/L no press, soft 450 g/L 15 min, firm 350 g/L 30 min, extra 280 g/L 45 min. These are common kitchen norms, not a recipe - follow your coagulant's instructions and food-safety guidance.
